Where the loss lives

Of 259 labelled fraud rings active in the evaluation window, 49 reach an analyst's top-50 queue. This is the stage-by-stage account of the other 210 — and of which single stage is worth fixing.

source data/funnel.json · data/funnel.csv run 34 generation runs rank_k 50 rings 259
01 / THE STORY IN ONE LINE

The funnel diagnoses. The re-ranker treats. The paired bootstrap confirms.

Three artefacts, one argument. None of them is a claim on its own.

Diagnose

The funnel splits ring recall into four stages and names the expensive one: ranking, −43.6 points, larger than seeding and building combined.

Treat

A supervised re-ranker attacks exactly that stage and reaches p@10 0.2778 against the shipped hand-set blend's 0.0500 — trained on ground-truth ring labels, which a deployment does not have.

Confirm

Paired bootstrap over the same held-out cycles, not two separate runs: +0.2278 [+0.1167, +0.3500]. The interval excludes zero, so the lift survives resampling.

Caveat, in the same breath

The 0.2778 is a label-advantaged number: the model is fitted on ground-truth ring labels that no deployment has on day one. It is what these features support when the labels are perfect, and it is never quoted as a production figure. The re-ranker figures come from data/eval_oracle.json, not from the funnel run on this page.

02 / THE FUNNEL

Four stages, and where each one costs

Distinct rings, counted once if they clear a stage in any cycle. Percentages are of the 259 rings that are structurally reachable at all; the loss column is the drop in percentage points of ring recall.

The headline

Ranking is the single largest loss in the funnel, at 43.6 points — more rings are lost between built and ranked than at seeding and building put together. A candidate that structurally covers the ring already exists; the scorer does not put it in the top 50. On this same run the shipped scorer measures p@10 = 0.097 [0.068, 0.126] (data/funnel.json → metric_cis).

03 / PER TYPOLOGY

The build stage's 26 points are two typologies, not eight

Six of the eight ring shapes carry 77–96% of their seeded rings through the build stage. Two do not. The interpretation label is derived in code from each row's own build retention — built ÷ seeded — not from a hand-written per-typology lookup.

The three ranking-limited rows lose almost nothing at build and 55–58 points at ranking. The three ordinary attrition rows lose steadily at every stage. The two build-destroyed rows are a different failure entirely — and together they are the build stage's 26 points.

04 / THE NAMED BUG

STACK is found 30 times out of 30, and then thrown away

Nothing is wrong with finding these rings. STACK is seeded 30 of 30 — perfectly, the only typology on the page that is. All 30 were located by the seed rule; 21 of them never become a candidate. The step that discards them runs after discovery, in expansion or pruning. BIPARTITE is the same failure at 28 of 31 seeded and 5 built.

That makes it a bounded, well-localised bug hunt with a known input and a known output, and the highest-value remaining engineering task that is not the re-ranker. Whether it can be closed in the time remaining is genuinely uncertain — the two obvious knobs have already been ruled out by experiment (data/build_diagnosis_h3.json, data/build_diagnosis_h2_d500.json), so this is not a tuning pass. Naming it precisely is itself the deliverable.

Why it isn't a tuning pass, in the numbers that make it one: the failure splits roughly in half between two causes that want opposite fixes, at the shipped config (hops=2, max_degree=50, pre-prune counts, data/build_diagnosis_h2_d50.json):

typology seeded containment_fail dilution_fail
BIPARTITE 281216
STACK 301414

containment_fail — expansion never reached the ring at all; the fix is to expand harder. dilution_fail — expansion reached it, then buried it under neighbourhood noise until Jaccard fell below 0.3; the fix is to expand less and prune better. Both knobs have already been tried and both failed on this same pre-prune measurement: hops 2→3 made it worse (BIPARTITE built 0→0, STACK 2→1, as mean candidate size roughly doubled and best Jaccard fell); the hub-degree guard 50→500 changed nothing at all on either typology. That is why one knob cannot fix this — the two halves of the failure want opposite treatment, and the two treatments tried so far have made things worse or done nothing.
